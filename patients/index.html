Doctors My Agency is a CRM for doctors, clinics, and hospitals — not a public clinic finder. Your clinic manages patients inside their own system. Book through the link, WhatsApp, or website chat your clinic gave you.

How to book with your clinic

  1. Message your clinic on WhatsApp or their website chat
  2. Use the booking link your doctor or reception shared with you
  3. The clinic's AI receptionist replies 24/7 — books slots, answers FAQs, and logs everything for staff
