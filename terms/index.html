2. Service description

Doctors My Agency provides Clinic-OS — a SaaS platform for healthcare clinics that includes:

  • AI Receptionist — automated responses to patient WhatsApp messages using AI (currently powered by DeepSeek or compatible LLM APIs)
  • Appointment management — AI-assisted booking, rescheduling, cancellation, and reminders
  • Patient CRM — contact records, appointment history, lead scoring, opt-out management
  • WhatsApp Business integration — connecting a clinic's own WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) via Meta Embedded Signup and the WhatsApp Cloud API
  • Staff portal — multi-user access for receptionists, nurses, and managers
  • Analytics & daily briefs — AI-generated clinic performance summaries
  • Missed-call recovery — automated WhatsApp follow-up for missed calls
  • Online booking page — public booking link at your clinic's custom slug

Features available depend on your plan. We reserve the right to add, modify, or discontinue features with reasonable notice.
