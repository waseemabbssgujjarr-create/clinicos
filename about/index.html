Meta Tech Partner — WhatsApp Business API

Official WhatsApp Business API integration via Meta Embedded Signup

Doctors My Agency is a registered Meta Tech Partner using the WhatsApp Business Cloud API. Every doctor connects their own WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) through Meta's official Embedded Signup flow — directly within the Clinic-OS dashboard. No third-party WhatsApp gateway is involved.

Our Meta App (Doctor's Agency, App ID: 19576489440​07520) is registered under the Messenger bots for business category and uses the following Meta platform permissions:

  • whatsapp_business_messaging — to send and receive messages on behalf of connected clinics
  • whatsapp_business_management — to read WABA IDs, phone numbers, and manage webhook subscriptions
  • business_management — to access business accounts during Embedded Signup asset resolution

We comply fully with WhatsApp's Business Policy, Meta Platform Terms, and all applicable data protection regulations. See our Privacy Policy for details on how WhatsApp data is handled.
