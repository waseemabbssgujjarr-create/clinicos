DMA AI — Hostinger Deployment

1. Extract this ZIP file.
2. Open the extracted folder and select all files, including .htaccess.
3. Upload them into your Hostinger public_html folder.
4. Make sure index.html is directly inside public_html, not inside another nested folder.
5. Open your domain and test:
   - the homepage
   - the contact form
   - the WhatsApp button
   - the Privacy overview and Service boundaries links

Important:
- Do not upload source files or node_modules; this package contains the production build only.
- The contact and newsletter forms use the EmailJS settings configured for this website.
- Do not include patient names, medical records, or sensitive health information in the enquiry form.
